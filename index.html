Ring Size Guide

Find your size
in a minute

1.  Take a ring that already fits the finger you have in mind.

2.  Measure straight across the inside of the band — that is the inner diameter, in millimetres.

3.  Match it on the chart — or slide to your measurement and we will size it for you.

Size 12 16.5 mm diameter · 51.8 mm around
